# Changelog

## v1.1

- Full screen now really fills the screen when you scroll down a page: every bar hides, including the toolbar, and a small set of pen tools sits on the side
- Fixed full screen not switching on at all in v1.0
- Each page can have its own page colour and header colour with the 🎨 button. The Look settings are now the defaults for new pages
- Scribble to delete has a sensitivity setting (Off, Strict, Normal, Easy) and Normal no longer rubs out handwriting with lots of up-and-down letters

## v1.0

First release of My Diary.

- Calendar home screen with symbols on each day and tap-to-enlarge days
- Five tabs per day: Affirmation, Gratitude, Diary, Lists and Appointments, plus a Period tab
- Over 40 ready-made templates, and a template maker to build your own
- Pen writing with pressure, highlighter, eraser, scribble to delete and 18 colours
- Keyboard typing on the page, a typing view for phones and the phone layout
- Select tool to loop around writing or typed text and move it
- Tap tick boxes to tick them, swipe a ticked row to delete it
- My lists folder for lists that aren't tied to a date, copy a list from another day and make a list from Excel
- Appointment alarms that ring even when the app is closed
- Period tracker with predictions, cycle history and reminders
- Dark mode, page colours and header colours
- Full screen while scrolling
- Phone and tablet syncing through your own Firebase project, plus backup and restore
